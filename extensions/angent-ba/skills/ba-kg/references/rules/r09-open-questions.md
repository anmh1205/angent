# R09: Decision-grade open questions

## Operational contract

Write a question only when the answer can change scope, actor rights, state transitions, test results, time/budget or a consequential architecture choice. Give options, affected IDs, risk and the decision owner if known.

## Checks before accepting an artifact

Keep inherited questions visible across linked documents and preserve the historical answer. A decision made in a brainstorm is not automatically ratified in SRS until source authority is established.

## Failure response

When independent requirements can proceed safely, mark the disputed section provisional rather than blocking all work with generic interviews.

## Grounded example

'Should committee approval require two independent members?' matters to workflow routing. 'Do you like a blue button?' may be a design preference rather than a blocking BA decision.

**Evidence boundary:** these checks govern specialist BA deliverables but do not independently authorize provider changes or claim a test ran. ANGENT Root and Execution retain task scope; Quality must inspect the specific output.
