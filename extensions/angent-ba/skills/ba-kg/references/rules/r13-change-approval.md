# R13: Scoped change and approval integrity

## Operational contract

Determine whether the user authorized research, documentation proposal, versioned edit or external write. A specialist workflow never enlarges that authority or invents a compulsory L1/L2 gate.

## Checks before accepting an artifact

Before editing, compare accepted source revision and current target, identify affected stable IDs and dependent artifacts, preserve unrelated changes and design a rollback for consequential updates.

## Failure response

After provider writes, re-read the modified target. If the provider cannot preserve versioned content or report real results, stop rather than fabricating success.

## Grounded example

A CR modifying a retry threshold does not authorize silently editing production timeout config, unrelated Jira status or customer Confluence pages.

**Evidence boundary:** specialist advice does not establish completed provider writes or approve business requirements. The actual source and observed result remain decisive; ANGENT Root and Quality retain oversight.
