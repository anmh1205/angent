# Compaction and isolation

## Compaction

Preserve:
- objective;
- constraints;
- verified progress;
- current decision;
- unresolved uncertainty;
- artifact/source pointers;
- next safe action.

Do not attempt to compress every detail into prose.

## Isolation

A specialist needs:
- bounded task;
- relevant AGENTS.md;
- required evidence/input;
- allowed effects;
- acceptance condition.

Do not broadcast the whole conversation to every worker.

At join, return evidence/artifacts and synthesize centrally.
