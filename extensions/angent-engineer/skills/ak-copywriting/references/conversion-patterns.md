# Channel-specific conversion patterns

AIDA (attention, interest, desire, action), PAS (problem, consequence, solution), BAB (current state, desired state, bridge), 4Ps (promise, picture, proof, action), 4Us (useful, specific, differentiated, time-sensitive when true), and FAB (feature, advantage, benefit) are different candidate structures, not universal rules or guaranteed uplift.

**Email:** Align subject, preview, body, supporting evidence and CTA with a single user decision. Welcome, nurture, onboarding, launch and re-engagement journeys have different timing and consent requirements; do not invent a send cadence, sender familiarity or time limit.

**Landing page:** Lead with a credible outcome, how it works, benefits, verifiable proof, objections/FAQ and a precise CTA. Reorder for audience awareness and evidence rather than enforcing a fixed block count.

**Headline/CTA:** Test distinct value propositions and objections, not just adjectives; the CTA states the next actual step and outcome. Do not invent ratings, logos, statistics or scarcity.

**Social/UI:** Social hooks must match their destination. Product UI copy prioritizes truthful status/action/recovery over persuasion. Preserve voice and meaning across language adaptation.
