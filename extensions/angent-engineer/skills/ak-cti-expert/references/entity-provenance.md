# CTI entity and provenance

For each material claim record:
- entity/indicator;
- source;
- observation date;
- confidence;
- identity-resolution ambiguity;
- whether the relation is direct or inferred.

Names/usernames/domains/wallets can collide or change ownership.

Do not merge identities solely because one identifier is similar.

Prefer defensive conclusions and actionable exposure/remediation over unnecessary personal-data collection.
