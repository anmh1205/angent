# Brand asset authenticity

For a real organization/product, locate the actual source of truth before generating or reconstructing brand-critical assets.

Prefer:
- user-supplied approved assets;
- project/repository brand files;
- official brand kit/site;
- connected design/source files.

Do not invent an "official" logo because no file was found.

If the actual logo/font/color cannot be established:
- ask for the missing asset when fidelity is required;
- or clearly treat the work as a proposed/reinterpreted direction rather than official reproduction.

## Rights and external references

A public image/reference can inform design without granting reproduction rights.

When copying/rebuilding a third-party visual, establish that the requested use is permitted and distinguish:
- mechanism/pattern reuse;
- close replication;
- direct asset reuse.

## Asset handoff

Preserve identity-critical information:
- original vector/raster source;
- approved variants;
- color/profile;
- intended background;
- safe area/minimum size;
- editable text/font dependency where relevant.

Mockups are not master assets.
