# Claim discipline

Keep claim type explicit internally and in wording when it matters.

## Observed

Directly inspected/reproduced in the current work.

Strongest grammar:
- “The current file contains…”
- “The test failed with…”
- “Provider state shows…”

## Prior/current external knowledge

Known from documentation/source that may have a version boundary.

Verify when the claim is load-bearing and freshness matters.

## Assumed

A working premise not yet established.

Do not let an assumption wear the grammar of observation.

## Inferred

A conclusion supported by observations plus a mechanism.

State residual uncertainty when another plausible mechanism remains.

## Confidence

Confidence rises because evidence discriminates alternatives—not because the explanation is long, repeated or fluent.
