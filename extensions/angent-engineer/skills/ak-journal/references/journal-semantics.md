# Journal semantics

A good journal entry records:
- context;
- intended outcome;
- action/attempt;
- observed result;
- artifact/source pointer;
- decision made;
- unresolved follow-up.

Later owners may use journal entries as evidence:
- Context may retrieve them;
- Learning may analyze repeated mechanisms;
- Reasoning may reconstruct history.

None should treat a journal sentence as current truth without checking freshness when the claim is time-sensitive.
