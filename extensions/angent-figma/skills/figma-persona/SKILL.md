# Build personas only from real user evidence

## When this method is justified
A persona synthesizes observed patterns of needs, jobs, constraints and behavior for a particular audience. It is not an invented name/age/photo or a stereotype of a market segment. If no suitable user research exists, deliver a research hypothesis/profile, explicitly not a validated persona.

## Research synthesis
1. Define who was studied, the sample and recruitment bias. Read actual interview notes, field observations, support tickets and analytics only when authorized. Do not interpolate missing demographics.
2. Tag evidence by recurring jobs, situations, motivations, accessibility needs, constraints, risk, workarounds and product relationship. Separate firsthand observations and inferred grouping.
3. Group patterns using meaningful behavioral differences, not role titles alone. Challenge exceptions and minority patterns that a convenient average would erase.
4. Name each persona by its job and operating context; include a human name or illustration only if it adds legitimate understanding and does not imply it is a real individual.
5. Compose persona card: audience scope, primary goals and jobs, typical situation, limitations, quote only if actually spoken and cited, pain points, decision criteria, product implications, counterexamples and confidence.
6. Draw/edit Figma cards using existing component/variable system when actual authorized provider tools support it; otherwise deliver an editable content schema, not a fabricated file edit.
7. Validate the artifact with the research owner: Can each material claim be traced? Are differences between personas consequential to design choices? Is the group sufficiently evidenced?

## Negative checks
No invented customer interviews, emotional ratings, photographic identity, demographic stereotypes or 'representative person' presented as statistically representative. Missing sample details are visible research gaps.

## Output
A small set of behaviorally distinct persona cards, a source-to-claim map, known sampling limitations and follow-up fieldwork questions. Integration with userflow/journey starts only after the persona hypotheses are accepted.
