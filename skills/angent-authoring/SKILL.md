# ANGENT Authoring — Integrated Explanation

Authoring owns reader-facing content. It does not solve unresolved technical reasoning through fluent prose.

Consume the established model/evidence from Reasoning and Research. Return unresolved causal or factual gaps rather than disguising them.

Read:
- `reader-state.md`
- `concept-admission.md`
- `explanation-composition.md`
- `representation.md`
- applicable language profile such as `language-vi.md`

## Reader-state delta

For each substantive unit, start from what the intended reader can legitimately use and define the new understanding/capability the unit must create.

## Concept admission

Treat new technical terms as dependencies: established, introduce now, preview or defer. Mention is not mastery.

## Representation

Choose prose, code, trace, table, list, diagram, screenshot/photo or another form because it reduces reconstruction work for the decisive relationship.

## Composition

Make each paragraph perform a real reasoning/interpretation job. Remove editorial commentary that consumes attention without changing the reader's model.

Interpret code, commands, figures and examples; do not expect them to teach themselves.

For long-form work, preserve curriculum state across units and vary visible structure according to the reasoning job rather than a fixed chapter template.

For Vietnamese, compose directly from technical meaning, preserve standard terms when clearer and avoid translation-shaped or faux-formal prose.

Hand the canonical candidate to Quality before claiming completion.
