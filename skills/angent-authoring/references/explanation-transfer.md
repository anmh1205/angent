# Transfer of understanding across answers, lessons and destinations

Use when a subject needs sustained technical explanation, when chapters build upon earlier lessons, or when a strong chat response degrades into a weak document. Do not load for a one-line fact, ordinary email or concise lookup.

## Preserve the reader's model

Carry forward the actual conceptual object and its interfaces, states, constraints and governing relation. Record only the prerequisite knowledge needed for the next inference; mentioning a concept is not evidence of understanding it. Determine what the reader can now explain, predict, diagnose or implement and what remains deferred.

An explanation becomes useful when a reader can connect an input or event to a mechanism and outcome. Introduce a component by its role, boundary and relation to the surrounding system. Follow it across a concrete trace, contrast or worked example without replacing the general rule by the example. When the mechanism is quantitative, derive or calculate; when it is procedural, show ordering and consequences; when spatial or temporal, choose a visual representation that clarifies the relation.

## Progress across units

A multi-unit document needs a stable progression, not identical unit templates. Early units establish language and first principles. Middle units deepen interacting mechanisms and expose failure cases. Advanced units combine earlier abilities under constraints, exceptions and design trade-offs. A later unit can revisit an earlier concept when it teaches a new relation rather than repeating the earlier overview.

Example: introducing a Zephyr ISR establishes what interrupt context is and which work is inappropriate there; later workqueue material traces the handoff to thread context and consequences of blocking; an advanced driver discussion examines concurrency, timing and data ownership across that handoff. Commands and snippets serve this mechanism. A chapter containing each keyword but no explanation of the handoff has not taught it.

Track prerequisite debt and accepted reader capabilities only when later work depends on them. A simple reference guide may legitimately remain compact and nonsequential.

## Transfer without a second authorship pass

Once prose and its subject relations are accepted, changing destination is a representation operation: preserve the meaning, paragraph dependencies, worked example and degree of certainty. Format may restructure blocks, link references and adjust figures; it may not regenerate text from bullet summaries. If a destination lacks an essential representation, return to Authoring rather than silently shortening or changing the mechanism.

For substantial multi-unit revisions, calibrate the working method against an accepted explanation, representative early/middle/advanced units, and an unlike-domain example. Inspect rendered code and diagrams, paragraph connections and a testable reader inference. A syntactically valid Notion page or long text count does not prove teaching quality.

## Keep the reader-facing prose natural

Orient a substantial answer to the supported conclusion, then develop the exact conceptual dependencies needed to understand it; close with the inference, decision or usable model that the evidence permits. Do not impose opening/ending paragraphs for short facts, generic coaching language for textbooks, or a visual in every section. When a reader remains confused, change the explanatory angle based on the missing mechanism; more paraphrases and lists are not automatically a repair.
