# Reader state

Reader-facing material is complete when it creates the promised capability, not when it exhausts the subject.

## Three distinct models

Do not collapse:

- **full cognitive/subject model** — everything needed for correctness;
- **active teaching model** — the relationships that must become usable for this reader now;
- **presented model** — the content and representation actually exposed in this unit.

The author may know twenty relevant constraints while the reader needs four now. Restricting the active set prevents overload; it does not require a short explanation.

## State

Distinguish:
- **established** — the reader can legitimately use the concept;
- **fragile** — known but needs repair for the current inference;
- **current gap** — missing distinction/mechanism/capability;
- **target state** — what the reader should be able to explain, predict, diagnose, compare, implement or verify afterward.

## Capability is a transition, not a chapter label

Record the distinction between *previewed* (recognizable name), *introduced* (definition and immediate role are available), *usable* (reader has enough mechanism to draw the target inference or operate on a representative case), and *fragile* (earlier exposure requires repair in a new context). A concept may be usable at one precision and remain incomplete at a deeper level. The next chapter must inherit the demonstrated level, not an inferred expert-level model from a heading or glossary entry.

If the current inference depends on a fragile or merely introduced concept, repair that relation before piling on downstream terminology. When the underlying relation is not established in the subject model, return to Reasoning/Research rather than using prose to hide the gap.

## Active cognitive job

A section should usually resolve one meaningful question, mechanism, distinction, decision or procedure.

The question need not be visible in formal prose.

## Concept admission

For a new term choose:
- established;
- introduce now;
- preview;
- defer.

Connect first use to something inspectable: object, behavior, operation, trace or contrast.

## Progressive precision

Introduce the minimum precise model needed now, then deepen when later reasoning depends on additional detail.

Progressive precision limits premature breadth, not the explanatory depth needed for the active relation.

## Closure

Do not stop because the point has been named or asserted once.

Stop when the target state is reached at the promised depth: the reader can use the relation for the intended nearby inference, prediction, diagnosis, comparison, implementation or verification.

More correct facts are not automatically more useful, but supporting examples, observations, contrasts and implementation details remain valuable when they are what makes the active relation understandable.

For long-form work, preserve reader state across chapters instead of recreating a generic beginner each time.
