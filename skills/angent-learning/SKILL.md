# ANGENT Learning — Experience to Understanding

Learning is broader than complaint handling and narrower than indiscriminate memory.

It improves future decisions without turning every incident into a permanent rule or user profile.

## Load references progressively

- references/experience-loop.md — capture experiences, infer/test mechanisms, promote carefully, and diagnose recurrence.
- references/memory-boundary.md — distinguish Learning from memory, AGENTS.md, collaboration state, and active task state.

## Experience intake

Capture when an event can plausibly improve future decisions:
- explicit correction;
- observed failure;
- repeatably successful strategy;
- rejected hypothesis;
- unexpected provider/tool behavior;
- repeated decision pattern;
- repeated working/collaboration pattern whose mechanism is identifiable;
- specialist finding exposing a reusable mechanism;
- unusually strong output with an identifiable cause.

Do not stop the main task to memorialize every event.

## Observation before explanation

Keep original evidence separate from causal interpretation.

A complaint or success proves that an outcome occurred, not why.

The user's quoted example is not automatically the whole lesson. Ask what generating behavior produced it and what other cases that behavior predicts.

## Infer a mechanism

Before generalizing:
1. reconstruct the decision/behavior that produced the outcome;
2. state the proposed mechanism;
3. compare a plausible alternative cause when material;
4. define applicability/boundary;
5. state a transfer prediction;
6. identify a counterexample/disconfirming case;
7. test before broad/consequential promotion.

## Working-method learning

Some durable lessons describe not a domain fact but a way of working that repeatedly improves decisions: how much autonomy is useful, when research should precede editing, what evidence standard prevents false completion, how to repair without overcorrection, or how explanation should expose relations.

Promote these only as causal working-method mechanisms with explicit applicability and counterconditions.

Do not encode “the user likes X” when the evidence actually supports “X works under condition Y because mechanism Z”.

Context may activate applicable working-method mechanisms into the current collaboration state. Learning itself does not choose the interaction mode for a task.

## Promote narrowly, but at the correct abstraction

Promote the smallest mechanism that explains the recurring behavior, not the smallest quoted symptom.

Do not paste user wording, project names, isolated disliked phrases, or one-off examples into global instructions.

When several examples are manifestations of one generating failure, promote one mechanism and use the examples as evidence/calibration cases.

A project-specific mechanism may remain project-local.

A success does not prove every workflow step was necessary.

## Recurrence diagnosis

When a promoted mechanism existed but the failure returns, do not merely strengthen the rule text.

Ask which layer failed:

- **retrieval** — the relevant mechanism/context was not recovered;
- **activation** — it was present but not recognized as applicable;
- **routing** — correction/work was sent to the wrong owner or too late;
- **ownership** — the mechanism was encoded under an owner that cannot control the behavior;
- **abstraction** — the stored lesson described a symptom rather than the real generator;
- **competition** — another instruction/state/model systematically overrode it.

Persistent recurrence is evidence about the system that applies the lesson, not just about the lesson wording.

## Retrieval and retirement

Load Learning guidance only when its boundary matches the current decision.

Later contradictory evidence may narrow, supersede, reopen, or retire a mechanism.

Do not keep an old rule merely because it was previously published.

## Relationship to memory and collaboration state

User/project facts belong to memory/context.

Task-relevant working relationship belongs to collaboration state assembled by Context.

Active work belongs to Continuity.

Learning owns reusable causal mechanisms that may inform future reasoning or collaboration.

Keep these classes separate even if they share storage infrastructure.
