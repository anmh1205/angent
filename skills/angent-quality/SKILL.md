# ANGENT Quality — Evidence-bound acceptance

Every output needs a proportionate **silent self-check** for task fit, still-binding context, warranted confidence, accuracy and readability. This is not a separate Quality skill call or formal verdict. Quality owns **explicit evidence-bound evaluation** when consequence, broad claims, accepted-baseline revision, recurrence or propagation risk warrants independent verification. It does not rewrite the answer, decide the task or grant authority.

## What a valid verdict means

Check relevant properties rather than filling a universal rubric:
- **Task fit:** the requested endpoint, active constraints, authorized effects and accepted baseline are preserved. Where a change is meant to create stakeholder value, distinguish deliverable conformity from evidence that the underlying need was actually served.
- **Reasoning and evidence:** decisive relations, alternatives and uncertainty are supported within actual source/version/scope boundaries. Tool/transport failures and search misses are not misreported as domain absence.
- **Execution and continuity:** consequential effects use current authority and a compatible mutation basis; ambiguous attempts are reconciled; resumed dependencies correspond to live state; observations support claimed outcomes.
- **Explanation:** accurate model, usable reader understanding, coherent paragraphs, depth/representation/language fit; technical documents do not regress from response-quality prose into outlines or generic commentary.
- **Coverage:** the actual inspected scope supports the claimed completeness. A representative sample cannot certify uninspected chapters/modules.
- **Revisions:** the intended defect improves **and** unrelated accepted strengths remain intact; recurrent complaints are repaired at their generating cause.

For a consequential PASS or FAIL, first check that the evaluator, criteria, evidence and test environment can support the judgment. Distinguish solution defect, evaluator defect, environment confound and unresolved attribution. Use BLOCKED if evidence cannot discriminate an important cause; do not force a binary verdict.

A required PASS supports only the dependent transition and its evidenced scope. Required FAIL/BLOCKED prevents accepting/publishing that candidate on the affected path; independent runnable work is not frozen. A low-risk direct answer still receives its implicit accuracy/fit check but needs no visible Quality ceremony.

## Evidence of value is not the same as a finished artifact

For value-oriented change, discriminate (1) authorized work completed, (2) solution characteristics met, and (3) benefit realized in the relevant stakeholder/context. A passed test, published document, visually polished design or completed ticket does not by itself establish operational impact. Choose the level of verification the Contract actually requires and the available evidence can establish. If benefits can only be observed after deployment, adoption or passage of time, state that separately; do not fail a sound artifact merely because post-adoption data do not yet exist, and do not invent an outcome metric or claim realized value. Assess competing stakeholder criteria using their real authority and requirements, not an averaged score invented by the evaluator.

For substantive response-authoring changes, compare what a reader understands from the opening alone, the opening with the ending, and the full explanation. Verify that closure integrates supported evidence rather than repeats the lead; preserve short-answer economy, depth, uncertainty, and chat-to-document parity. Use Authoring's `reader-orientation-and-closure.md` positive and negative cases as inputs, not a fixed-format checklist.

## Test the decisive claim at its proper boundary

A credible evaluation identifies the exact claim being accepted, the observation that would falsify it, and the scope in which the observation applies. For a proposed cross-layer repair, assess whether evidence separates the competing failure causes and whether the requested behavior passes the necessary integration boundaries; a source diff or passing isolated test cannot certify an unexercised device-to-service path. For a design choice, check whether the choice still stands under plausible decision-reversing assumptions, not just whether a pros/cons table exists. For an educational artifact, check an inference the reader should now be able to make, not how many concepts were named. Report source inspection, simulated prompts, live activated runs, destination read-back and real-user outcomes as distinct evidence levels.

## Evaluate report and answer arguments

A well-formed paragraph does not guarantee that the report's overall conclusion is warranted. For analytic work, verify that the opening matches the observed state and scope; central claims have direct support or explicitly bounded inference; alternative explanations are considered when decisive; severity is supported; and recommendations address an established cause or constraint. An executive summary cannot be more confident than the evidence-bearing body.

For chat, evaluate accessibility of the answer, continuity of the underlying mechanism and a genuine conclusion without rewarding verbosity. For long-form reports and teaching materials, inspect early, middle and late units and the destination render when relevant. Identify concrete missing or misleading relations rather than grading a fixed heading formula. Use `references/report-evaluation.md` for claim-to-decision checks.

## Choose an oracle that can reject a plausible wrong result

Before relying on a green check, identify a plausible wrong result that would also pass it. If a package inventory passes while a resource points to the wrong method, add a resource-identity check; if a report contains every topic but cannot justify its recommendation, check the deciding relation. Select an observation that separates those cases. Do not expand a routine task into a universal test matrix.

Relative improvement and absolute acceptance are separate judgments. A candidate may remove two defects and retain another required failure; record the gain and residual defect while keeping dependent acceptance failed. If the evaluator or environment cannot distinguish the cause, preserve that attribution limit instead of assigning a product failure by default.

## Calibrate changes, not arbitrary counts

For a change to a widely used skill, method or multi-unit writing approach, compare representative failure, accepted-success and counter-cases where feasible. Inspect observable outputs/trajectories and protected properties, not heading counts, forbidden words, source counts or a self-reported score. One successful test demonstrates possibility, not whole-system reliability.

When a defect appears, return it to its **earliest generating owner**: Contract/Context for wrong obligations or provenance, Reasoning/Research for a weak model/evidence, Execution/Continuity for effects/state, Authoring for expression, Format for destination rendering. Quality does not patch the candidate internally.

Store a reproducible pointer to materially reused behavioral acceptance evidence when appropriate; do not introduce a separate grading platform.
