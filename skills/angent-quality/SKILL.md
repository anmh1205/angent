# ANGENT Quality — Evidence-bound acceptance

Quality evaluates when the task consequence, a broad claim, revision of accepted work, recurring defect or propagation risk makes verification materially useful. It does not rewrite the answer, decide the task, grant authority or require a gate for every routine reply.

## What a valid verdict means

Check relevant properties rather than filling a universal rubric:
- **Task fit:** the requested endpoint, active constraints, authorized effects and accepted baseline are preserved. Where a change is meant to create stakeholder value, distinguish deliverable conformity from evidence that the underlying need was actually served.
- **Reasoning and evidence:** decisive relations, alternatives and uncertainty are supported within actual source/version/scope boundaries. Tool/transport failures and search misses are not misreported as domain absence.
- **Execution and continuity:** consequential effects use current authority and a compatible mutation basis; ambiguous attempts are reconciled; resumed dependencies correspond to live state; observations support claimed outcomes.
- **Explanation:** accurate model, usable reader understanding, coherent paragraphs, depth/representation/language fit; technical documents do not regress from response-quality prose into outlines or generic commentary.
- **Coverage:** the actual inspected scope supports the claimed completeness. A representative sample cannot certify uninspected chapters/modules.
- **Revisions:** the intended defect improves **and** unrelated accepted strengths remain intact; recurrent complaints are repaired at their generating cause.

For a consequential PASS or FAIL, first check that the evaluator, criteria, evidence and test environment can support the judgment. Distinguish solution defect, evaluator defect, environment confound and unresolved attribution. Use BLOCKED if evidence cannot discriminate an important cause; do not force a binary verdict.

A required PASS supports only the dependent transition and its evidenced scope. Required FAIL/BLOCKED prevents accepting/publishing that candidate on the affected path; independent runnable work is not frozen. A low-risk direct answer does not need a ceremony merely because Quality exists.

## Evidence of value is not the same as a finished artifact

For value-oriented change, discriminate (1) authorized work completed, (2) solution characteristics met, and (3) benefit realized in the relevant stakeholder/context. A passed test, published document, visually polished design or completed ticket does not by itself establish operational impact. Choose the level of verification the Contract actually requires and the available evidence can establish. If benefits can only be observed after deployment, adoption or passage of time, state that separately; do not fail a sound artifact merely because post-adoption data do not yet exist, and do not invent an outcome metric or claim realized value. Assess competing stakeholder criteria using their real authority and requirements, not an averaged score invented by the evaluator.

## Calibrate changes, not arbitrary counts

For a change to a widely used skill, method or multi-unit writing approach, compare representative failure, accepted-success and counter-cases where feasible. Inspect observable outputs/trajectories and protected properties, not heading counts, forbidden words, source counts or a self-reported score. One successful test demonstrates possibility, not whole-system reliability.

When a defect appears, return it to its **earliest generating owner**: Contract/Context for wrong obligations or provenance, Reasoning/Research for a weak model/evidence, Execution/Continuity for effects/state, Authoring for expression, Format for destination rendering. Quality does not patch the candidate internally.

Store a reproducible pointer to materially reused behavioral acceptance evidence when appropriate; do not introduce a separate grading platform.
