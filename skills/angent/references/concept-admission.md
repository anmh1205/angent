# Concept admission — control prerequisite debt

Technical terms are dependencies in an explanation.

## States

- **established** — enough meaning exists to reason with it;
- **introduce now** — the unit must create usable meaning before relying on it;
- **preview** — naming may orient the reader but the term cannot yet be a premise;
- **defer** — correct but unnecessary for the current inference.

## Meaningful first use

Connect a new term to something the reader can already inspect or understand: a concrete object, observable behavior, familiar operation, worked trace or discriminating contrast.

Acronym expansion, translation, glossary link or parenthetical synonym does not establish a concept by itself.

## Fan-out

Multiple new names are acceptable when their relationship is itself the learning target and the reader has an anchor. Otherwise stage them.

This is not a term-count rule. Expert reference material can be intentionally dense.

## Progressive precision

Introduce the minimum precise model needed for the current capability, then deepen it when later reasoning depends on the additional detail.
