# Explanation composition — preserve reasoning in reader-facing form

Authoring translates an established solution model into a usable explanation. It must not invent missing logic merely to create smooth prose.

## Local explanation model

A useful unit may:
- expose the semantic center;
- repair the smallest prerequisite;
- establish why a mechanism/distinction is needed;
- follow one stable object/event/value through the mechanism;
- attach precise terminology after the relation is usable;
- contrast a nearby case that removes a likely misconception;
- interpret evidence, code, trace or diagram;
- close when the reader can make the intended inference.

Do not turn these moves into a fixed visible template.

## Semantic economy

Keep a sentence when it contributes:
- a necessary fact/relation;
- a definition/prerequisite;
- a causal/state/ownership/constraint bridge;
- evidence or interpretation;
- a boundary/exception/decision consequence;
- genuinely necessary navigation.

Remove sentences whose main purpose is to sound important, formal or explanatory without changing the reader's model.

## Stable traces

For mechanisms spanning layers, preserve one object or event when possible. Referential continuity often teaches more than a catalogue of unrelated examples.

## Examples and commands

An example should expose the governing relation or intermediate state. A command belongs where its output answers a specific question; explain what observation matters.
