# ANGENT — Coordination at real boundaries

Skills normally share one working context. There is **no universal handoff object, state machine, intermediate document or mandatory owner sequence**. Coordination exists to prevent information loss where a transition matters, not to make simple work formal.

## The minimum transferable context

At a consequential boundary, carry only what the recipient cannot safely reconstruct:

1. **Intent and limits:** the requested outcome, active exclusions/obligations, current authority where an effect is proposed.
2. **Grounding:** the decisive facts, their actual sources/versions, uncertainty and any accepted baseline that must be preserved.
3. **Next work:** what decision, explanation, observation or bounded effect is needed, with dependencies when material.
4. **Validation:** what would confirm the result, invalidate the premise or require reconciliation.

Omit irrelevant fields. A direct answer or ordinary same-agent tool call usually needs no handoff at all. References and concise prose are sufficient; do not produce TaskModel/Delta/Envelope/YAML by default.

## Boundaries that change correctness

- **Research → Reasoning → Authoring:** preserve load-bearing domain relations and their evidence limits. Do not hand Authoring a flat topic inventory or ask it to invent missing mechanisms. Authoring chooses presentation and reader depth; Research does not dictate chapter order.
- **Reasoning → Execution:** pass the authorized effect, concrete target, preconditions and expected observation when consequential. Revalidate authorization at invocation and refresh an existing-state mutation basis if relevant. A tool reply is not automatically endpoint evidence.
- **Execution → Reasoning:** pass what was actually observed, the failure/transport boundary, identity, scope and remaining uncertainty. A missing search result or tool error is not global evidence of absence.
- **Pending effect → scheduling/Continuity:** only when work is genuinely asynchronous or interruption-sensitive, retain operation handle, relevant dependency, WAITING_EXTERNAL status and next informative observation condition. Do not invent a polling scheduler.
- **Correction/Quality → responsible owner:** carry the observed defect, accepted strengths, causal hypothesis, affected scope and evidence for repair. A required FAIL/BLOCKED prevents dependent acceptance, not unrelated work.
- **Delegation:** attenuate the delegated task and effect scope; pass only relevant trustworthy context. A specialist return is evidence/proposal, not an authority upgrade.
- **Long-form learning:** preserve accepted reader capability and prerequisite/artifact pointers only when later units depend on them. Mentioning a concept or writing a page does not prove comprehension.
- **Resume:** saved identifiers, decisions and baselines are hints until reconciled with current sources. Do not replay an ambiguous write.

These boundaries are conditional. Detailed effect, validation, curriculum and resume rules live with Execution, Quality, Authoring and Continuity respectively; this file does not duplicate their procedures.
