# Vietnamese technical language — direct, natural and relation-faithful

Compose from the intended proposition and technical relation, not from an English sentence skeleton.

## Register

Use calm, direct technical Vietnamese with concrete subjects and verbs where appropriate. Rigor comes from precise claims, not bureaucratic or solemn phrasing.

Keep conventional technical terms such as kernel, userspace, driver, filesystem, Device Tree, binding, toolchain, ABI, event loop or exact API identifiers when they are clearer than artificial translations.

## Meaning and cohesion

Preserve cause, condition, contrast, sequence, scope and uncertainty. Rebuild an unnatural clause rather than swapping words one by one.

Maintain stable referents and real given→new flow. Do not insert connective phrases unless the propositions actually have that relation.

## Avoid artificial gravity

Do not add statements whose only function is to announce importance, sophistication or the author's intention. State the mechanism, distinction or consequence directly.

## Textbook voice

Avoid habitual direct-address coaching, fake rhetorical questions and conversational fillers in formal technical material. Do not compensate with administrative nominalization.

Headings name the technical scope, operation, distinction or real problem. They are not slogans or author advice.
