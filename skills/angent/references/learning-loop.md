# Learning loop — convert experience into transferable mechanisms

Learning should improve future decisions without turning every incident into a global rule.

## Evidence classes

Keep distinct:
- user correction;
- observed failure;
- observed success;
- provider/runtime outcome;
- source-grounded fact;
- rejected hypothesis;
- model-authored causal explanation;
- transfer/negative-control result.

## From experience to understanding

For a candidate lesson:
1. identify the observed outcome;
2. reconstruct the decision that produced it;
3. state the causal mechanism;
4. compare a plausible alternative explanation;
5. define applicability and boundary;
6. predict a new case where the mechanism should transfer;
7. state a counterexample or disconfirming observation;
8. test before promotion when the lesson is broad/consequential.

## Success learning

Do not learn only from complaints. A repeatably successful strategy can reveal a useful mechanism, but separate:
- success because of the strategy;
- success despite the strategy;
- success caused by an uncontrolled factor.

## Promotion

Promote the smallest mechanism to the owner responsible for the decision. Preserve evidence separately. Retire narrower guards that the new mechanism subsumes.

A local defect can remain local. Generalization requires a reason.

## Reopening

Later recurrence or contradiction reopens the lesson. Do not protect an old rule merely because it was previously published.
