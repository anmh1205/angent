# Quality rubric — verify the solution before the presentation

Quality is an independent gate.

## Layer A — solution integrity

PASS requires the relevant items below:

1. **Task fit** — solves the actual outcome within scope/authorization.
2. **Model integrity** — important actors, relations, constraints and states are not omitted or invented.
3. **Evidence sufficiency** — material conclusions are supported at the required precision.
4. **Alternative handling** — plausible competing explanations/options are considered when they could change the result.
5. **Causal integrity** — causal claims are not inferred from chronology or correlation alone.
6. **Uncertainty calibration** — unresolved uncertainty remains visible in the decision.
7. **Decision integrity** — selected action follows from constraints/consequences, not a generic feature inventory.
8. **Plan integrity** — dependencies, replan conditions and verification points are coherent.
9. **Coverage integrity** — whole-scope claims have whole-scope evidence; samples are labeled as samples.
10. **Endpoint integrity** — operational completion is proven by the authoritative state required by the Contract.

When this layer fails, return the earliest faulty decision to Reasoning, Research or Execution.

## Layer B — communication integrity

For reader-facing work evaluate:
1. reader-state fit;
2. concept admission;
3. explanatory progress/semantic economy;
4. representation fit;
5. structure/prose integrity;
6. terminology/language;
7. evidence/example interpretation;
8. promised capability closure;
9. long-form transfer where applicable.

Return these failures to Authoring.

A fluent explanation of an invalid solution fails. A valid solution may also fail when the user cannot reliably interpret or use it.
