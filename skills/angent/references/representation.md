# Representation — minimize reconstruction work

Choose representation as part of reasoning about communication, not as decoration after prose.

## Common matches

- **prose** — a small number of claims linked by cause, constraint, ownership or interpretation;
- **flow diagram** — ordered transformations/control or data paths;
- **sequence/timeline** — temporal interaction;
- **state diagram** — lifecycle and allowed transitions;
- **topology/dependency graph** — connectivity, hierarchy or dependency;
- **table** — entities compared on shared explicit axes;
- **bullets** — genuinely parallel peers or bounded alternatives;
- **numbered procedure** — ordered actions are the communicative job;
- **code/source** — exact implementation/syntax is evidence;
- **trace/log/output** — runtime observation is evidence;
- **chart** — quantitative relation/trend;
- **photo/screenshot/schematic** — exact physical/UI state or captured evidence.

## Probes

**Reconstruction cost:** would another form make the decisive relation inspectable instead of mentally reconstructed?

**Deletion:** if the representation disappears, is meaningful structure lost? If not, it may be decoration.

**Interpretation:** can the reader tell what conclusion to draw?

**Truth:** every arrow, grouping, sequence and axis must correspond to a real relation.

Mermaid is useful for abstract flow/state/sequence/dependency relations, not as a substitute for authentic physical evidence, schematics, waveforms or screenshots.
